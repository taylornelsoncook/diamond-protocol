# Diamond Protocol

The Diamond Protocol coaching platform: client accounts, monthly subscriptions and billing, in-person payments at your facility, in parks and at clients' homes (Tap to Pay on iPhone, front-desk reader, card on file, cash), session packs and check-ins, workout programming with demo videos, a client phone app, and an open API with webhooks.

**Start with `CHECKLIST.md`** for the steps only you can do (Stripe, Apple, hosting).

This is the working model. It runs on your computer with no installs beyond Node.js, and every screen is wired to a real database.

## Run it

You need **Node.js 22.13 or newer** (check with `node -v`; download from nodejs.org).

```bash
cp .env.example .env      # then change ADMIN_PASSWORD
npm run seed              # creates your login plus sample plans, programs and clients
npm start                 # open http://localhost:3000
npm test                  # runs the full test suite (519 tests)
```

The seed prints your login and a sample client app link. To start over, delete the `data` folder and seed again.

## What's in it

**Coach dashboard** (`/`)
- **Today:** monthly recurring revenue, active clients, failed payments, workouts logged this week, a list of what needs you (failed charges, trials ending, clients who've gone quiet) and a live activity feed.
- **Clients:** search and filter; create an account, start a plan and assign a program in one step; pause, resume, cancel, change plan; payment history with retry; private app link; a short profile note. **Active clients** (the Today tile and the list's Active filter mean the same thing) are those not archived whose membership is paid up or on a free trial.
- **Archive:** owners and coaches archive a client who stopped training (front desk can't). They leave the client list, search, pickers, rosters, dashboard counts and every automatic message (reminder texts, open-spot offers, review asks, announcement emails, athletes to check on); nothing is deleted. A client with a membership can't be archived until it's canceled; upcoming bookings and standing spots are canceled (credits back, payments refunded) after a second confirmation. Find them under Clients → Archived, or from the hint when a search only matches archived clients, and bring them back from their page.
- **Staff notes:** dated notes on a client with the author's name. Anyone on staff adds notes; authors change their own; owners delete any note and pin any; others delete their own. Pinned notes show at the top of the client page. Coach-only notes are never shown to front desk and never go to a family (they're left out of the family's data download). Other notes are in the download and removed when the family is deleted.
- **Billing:** plans with live revenue per plan, invoice history, retry failed charges (a retry you start, or a parent starts from the portal, never counts toward the 5 tries before a membership is canceled; each invoice lists every card charge tried and who started it). If the bank approves a charge after the invoice was already paid another way (pay link, cash or check, another charge) or voided, it's refunded automatically, once, and you get an email and a "needs your attention" item on Today. In test mode, run the billing clock for a future date to watch trials convert, renewals charge and retries happen.
- **Point of sale:** pick where you are and who you're with, tap products into the sale, and charge by Tap to Pay on iPhone, front-desk reader, card on file or cash. Optionally save the tapped card for future payments. Refunds (full or partial) and a live list of recent sales. Only the owner gives discounts (coaches and front desk see "Ask the owner"; the owner can allow staff a limit in setup). Setup covers locations, products (sessions, packs, gear) and readers.
- **Inventory:** gear can count its stock, per size (Youth M, S, M, L...). The counter asks which size and shows how many are left. Sales take stock out, full refunds put it back, and the Inventory screen records deliveries, shelf counts and adjustments with who did it. Anything at or below its low-stock number shows on Today and in the Monday summary. Front desk can record deliveries and counts.
- **Book now page** (`/book`): upcoming classes, clinics and camp days with open spots, an age filter and the next evaluation times, for the website, Instagram bio and Google profile. Families sign in or sign up to book. Put it on any website with one line (`<script src=".../embed.js" async></script>`, or `data-button="Book now"` for a button). No names are shown. Link and code are under Leads → Settings; owners can turn it off.
- **Google review requests:** after an athlete's 10th session, or a personal best on a testing day the family can see, the parent gets one friendly email asking for a Google review. At most once every 6 months per family, never to a family behind on a payment or asking to be deleted, only between 10 am and 7 pm, with a "don't ask again" link. Off until the owner pastes their Google review link under Leads → Settings, which also shows how many were sent and opened.
- **Group messages** (Leads → Group messages, owners only): emails or texts to all families, members, lapsed members, families without a membership, families whose trial ended or leads by stage, narrowed by athlete age and sport. Shows who it reaches as you type, sends a test to you, and sends only after confirming the number. `{first_name}` fills in the parent's name, links are counted when clicked, and each email ends with the business address and a "stop these emails" link (a button press, so email scanners can't unsubscribe anyone). Stopped addresses also get no review requests.
- **Two-way messages:** athletes (in their app) and parents (in the portal) can write back under "Messages with your coach". Only coaches see replies. The coach who last wrote to that athlete gets an email (the owners, if no coach has written yet), replies show on Today until a coach opens the athlete's page, and families see "Seen" once they have. Up to 20 replies a day per athlete.
- **Video library:** your own exercise videos, stored in your Cloudflare R2 bucket and played from your own address, so they load fast and never come from another site. `tools/upload-videos.mjs` runs once on the computer with the videos: each file becomes an exercise named after it (a folder named after a category sets it), gets a quick-loading copy and a still picture, and is uploaded; the same name in two folders goes up once, and a stopped run carries on. It writes a list the owner brings in at Settings → Exercise library → Import a list (checked first, all or nothing; exercises already in the library are left, given the video where they have none, or given the new one). Videos show their still until played, and the library shows 60 at a time with search. See DEPLOY.md → Video library.
- **Build a program from a PDF** (Programs → Build from a PDF, owners and coaches): choose a PDF of a program, or a clear photo of a printed one. Claude reads it into a draft in the program builder's format: weeks and days, each exercise with its sets and reps (like 3 × 8), a weight as a percent of a tested back squat, bench or clean max when the file gives one, and notes (tempo, supersets) kept beside each line. Each exercise is matched to the library only when it's the same name (abbreviations like RDL and DB, and plurals, count) and marked "Matched"; a close one is only offered (press Use it, or Use all after looking them over); anything else is offered as a new exercise. Anything Claude wasn't sure about is listed at the top. You change what you need and save it as a new program or as weeks added to one; nothing is saved until then, and every problem is listed first. Adding to a program puts the file's weeks after its last week. Needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; a typical program costs well under a dollar to read (a long PDF more). Up to 100 pages or a 3.7 MB photo per file, 20 files an hour per person and 150 a day in all.
- **Dictate a workout** (Programs → Dictate a workout, owners and coaches): say or type a workout the way you would tell an athlete ("back squat four by five at seventy five percent, two minutes rest, superset with RDL three by eight"), press Speak to talk in browsers that can listen, and Claude turns it into the same checkable draft as Build from a PDF: exercises matched to your library, sets, reps, tempo, rest, effort, loads and supersets in place, for you to fix and save as a new program or as a day added to one.
- **Weights from test results:** in a program, set an exercise to a percent of the athlete's back squat, bench press or power clean max. The athlete app shows the weight worked out from their latest shared max, rounded to 5 lb (for example "155 lb (75% of your 205 lb back squat max)"), and it updates on its own after the next testing day is shared.
- **Lighter days after a rough check-in:** the athlete app reads today's daily check-in and says above the workout whether to train as written, go a little lighter (one problem such as soreness 4 of 5: weights from a max come down 10 points) or take an easy day (two or more problems, under 5 hours of sleep or soreness 5 of 5: down 20 points and one set less). Coaches see the suggestion next to the check-in on Today and can turn it off in Schedule → Hours & settings. With a WHOOP or Oura linked (or a file imported), last night's recovery, sleep and HRV count too, under rules the coaches set in Schedule → Hours & settings (how low is too low, how many points come off, how many sets).
- **Progression that earns itself:** when an athlete hits every planned set at the top of the rep range two workouts in a row, the app suggests the next step (10 lb on lower-body lifts, 5 lb on upper-body, or a rep a set where no weight is logged). Coaches approve or hold it from Today or the athlete's Training panel, add a step by hand, undo one, or set it to apply on its own. Approved steps show in the athlete's workout ("+10 lb from your progression").
- **Live session view:** open a session from Schedule while it's on and the Live panel shows every athlete: here or not, today's readiness, the workout they're on with their own weights and sets, and what they've logged, refreshing on its own. A coach can swap an exercise for one athlete on the spot (a sore knee, no rack), for that workout or the rest of their program; the athlete's app and the weight-room screen show the swap right away and the plan itself is untouched.
- **Supersets that flow:** in the athlete app, a superset or circuit runs as one flow: log a set of A1 and A2 opens straight away, and the rest timer starts only when the round is done, so the phone follows the way the workout is actually run.
- **Swaps athletes may pick:** on any exercise in the library, list the stand-ins an athlete may choose on their own (no barbell, knee, at home…). In the app, "Can't do this today?" offers that list; the pick is for that workout only, the coach sees it on the session's Live panel, and a coach's own swap always wins.
- **Warm-up and cool-down blocks:** write a block once on the Programs page (a name and a few moves with what to do), attach it to any workout from the builder, and it follows the workout into the athlete app, onto the weight-room screen and onto the session's Live panel. Change the block and every workout shows the change.
- **Monthly parent reports:** once a month ends, a report is written for each athlete from what they logged: workouts against the program's pace, sessions attended, check-ins, the estimated one-rep max trend per lift, steps approved. Coaches add a line and send it from Programs → Monthly reports (Today reminds them), or the owner lets them go out on their own in the first week of the month. Parents read them in the portal's Progress tab and by email. No money in them.
- **Skill badges:** coaches award badges for skills an athlete has shown ("Sprint start", "Hinge pattern"), from the athlete's page, with an optional note. New badges can be made on the spot, with a category (Speed, Strength, Power, Mobility, Skill, Mindset). Athletes and parents see them at the top of the Performance tab, and the family gets an email. Milestones show there on their own too: 10, 25, 50+ sessions or workouts, a 7, 14 or 30-day check-in streak, and PRs. A badge given by mistake can be taken back. Front desk can look but not award.
- **Weight-room screen:** a TV or shared tablet in the weight room, opened with the "weight-room screen" link shown when a check-in tablet is set up (`/tv#<key>`). A coach picks the workout on the session page; from 30 minutes before the start the screen shows it in big type. Athletes tap their name (first name and last initial) to see their own weights from their tested max (lighter after a rough check-in) and log the workout, which also checks them in. It counts toward their program when the workout is part of it, and toward streaks and goals either way.
- **Outside data (Settings → Data import):** bring in an athlete's numbers from any system. Pick where the file comes from (WHOOP, Oura, Garmin Connect, Apple Health, Fitbit, Strava, TrainingPeaks) and the menu says how to get the export; Apple Health and Fitbit zips go in as they are, and any other spreadsheet's columns can be saved as the same measures a wearable gives. WHOOP exports (`physiological_cycles.csv`, `sleeps.csv`, `workouts.csv`) are recognized as they are; any other CSV, Excel (.xlsx), Google Sheets link (shared "Anyone with the link") or PDF with a real table of text is matched up once (which column is the date, which numbers to keep, their names and units). You pick the athlete; the file is checked first and nothing saves until every problem is fixed. Owners and coaches bring files in for any athlete, parents for their own from the portal's Progress tab; front desk can look. Recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and strain show as cards with a 7-day and 30-day average and a small chart on the client page, the parent portal and the athlete app, with everything else in a table and the latest wearable workouts. Undo removes what one import saved. For now it's only shown: it doesn't change workouts.
- **Lesson quizzes and course certificates:** coaches add a multiple-choice quiz to any lesson as plain text (a question, then choices starting with `-`, the right one with `*`). Athletes, or a parent with them, need 80% to finish the lesson; wrong questions are marked but the answers are never shown, and they can try again. Finishing every lesson in a course issues a certificate once: the family gets an email with a link (`/certificate#<token>`) to print or share, it's listed on the Education tab, and the Education screen counts certificates per course.
- **Parent education:** courses can be marked "for parents" with an optional age range. They show on the parent portal's Home under "For parents" (only when a child is in the age range), never in the athlete app, and can't be assigned to athletes. Parents mark lessons read, and the Education screen shows how many parents are reading. Three starter drafts (growth spurts and training, fueling a young athlete, recruiting basics) can be added with one tap and edited before publishing.
- **Online store:** owners put a price on any program or athlete course and turn on "Sell online" (on the program page, or Sell online on the Education screen). Families buy from the parent portal's Programs tab with the family card; out-of-town athletes find everything at `/shop` and sign up (or sign in) to buy. A bought program goes straight into the athlete's app, even without a membership, and the family gets an email with the app link. A course for sale shows locked until it's bought; athletes a coach assigns it to, and anyone who had already started it, keep it open. A full refund of the sale ends access. Programs → Online store shows what's for sale, what sold, and the link to share.
- **How each client trains:** staff tag each client Hybrid athlete, In-facility or Remote client. The tag shows under their name and filters the client list and export.
- **Changing a membership:** owners change a plan to start now, wait until the membership renews, or start now and charge the price difference for the rest of the month. A waiting change shows on the client page, in Billing and in the parent portal, and can be canceled.
- **Refunds report:** Billing → Refunds lists every refund (memberships, sales and automatic "paid twice" refunds) with the athlete, email and phone, by date, with a CSV.
- **Payment lockout:** when a membership payment declines on the first charge and 3 retries, about 9 days (the owner can change or turn this off), for every client, the family can only update the card and pay in the portal; the app, online booking and self check-in wait until it's paid. Staff can still book and check them in.
- **Education tabs:** Athlete education, Parent education, Coach's education, Blogs and Research. Coach's education is also public at `/learn`.
- **Filling open spots:** Today lists every class families book single spots in (group classes, clinics and camp days sold by the day; not privates, evaluations or team sessions) that has a spot left in the next 7 days, soonest first and grouped by day, whoever leads it: coach, place, booked of capacity, spots left, how many families fit (the right age, not booked, and a regular of that class, a member, or someone who came in the last 45 days) and the offers sent so far. Coaches see every coach's classes too (no prices) and can tick "My classes only". One tap emails the families who fit, and texts families who turned texts on, a link (`/spot/<token>`) that books without signing in. The first to tap gets the spot, covered by their membership or pack, or paid with the family card. Each family gets at most 2 offers a day. When offers can't go out (starts in under 2 hours, families on the waitlist, nobody fits, offers turned off) the row says why. Owners can switch it to automatic (group classes and clinics, between 10 am and 7 pm, within 30 hours of the start) or turn offers off; the list still shows.
- **Trial offers ("try this session for $X", owner only):** from any row on Today the owner picks a price (the drop-in to start, up to the drop-in or $200 with none; $0 is free), how many families (up to 30) and the message, with a live preview. Families who fit get it, plus families who had a standard offer for that class and haven't booked (their link switches to the new price). Booking from the link charges that price to the family card, or books free at $0; a membership that covers the class still covers it. The price applies only to that link, once, until the session starts. Open leads without a family account aren't sent it (booking needs an account and a signed waiver); the dialog says how many. Each send is in the activity feed and the audit log, and the row shows "Trial offer sent at $X". Coaches can't send trial offers (the server refuses).
- **Coaches panel (owner only):** on Today, each active coach and owner who leads sessions: today's sessions and the next one, the next 7 days (sessions, how full their classes are, privates booked), attendance at what they led in the last 7 days and upcoming days off, with a link to their schedule (Schedule → Show: a coach). Sessions with no coach in the next 7 days are flagged.
- **Progress notes for parents:** on a testing day, Draft notes writes a few plain-English sentences per athlete from the results: the biggest improvements and new bests, first-time tests, anything that dipped (with a word about growth spurts when that applies) and what's next. It uses the athlete's first name, never he or she. Coaches edit and approve each one (or approve all after reading them). Parents see the approved note under "From your coach" on the report and in the portal once the day is shared, and it goes in the results email. With `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set, Claude rewords each draft first, using only the results; if that fails, the plain draft is used.
- **Daily money checks:** each morning (after 6 am) the app checks the day before for possible double charges (the same athlete charged the same amount within 10 minutes, or a membership month paid twice), a refund spike, and payments stuck waiting for a card. With Stripe connected it also matches every card payment one by one: money Stripe took that the app has no record of, payments the app shows as paid that Stripe doesn't have, and amounts that don't agree. Anything found is emailed to the owners and shown on Billing until someone marks it as looked at. If Stripe can't be reached the check tries again each hour, and the owners hear about it after 6 failed tries. Owners can re-check any of the last 90 days.
- **Schedule:** weekly group classes, camps, clinics, team sessions and one-offs, with age ranges, capacity, prices and a coach. A class's coach leads every session; change one session's coach for a sub (the rest of the class keeps its coach), or hand the whole class over and every upcoming session follows except those with a sub. Session rows, the session page and Today's sessions show the coach; "My sessions only" on the Schedule shows just what you lead. Weekly classes are scheduled 8 weeks ahead automatically. Each session has a roster with one-tap check-in, how every athlete is covered (member, credit, paid, registered or unpaid), a medical flag, a waitlist, and Collect (card on file, cash or Tap to Pay) for anyone unpaid. Canceling a session returns credits, refunds drop-ins and emails families. Moving a session emails booked families and waitlisted ones ("still on the waitlist"), once each. Giving a coach a session, class or hours at a time they already lead something (anywhere) or are off shows the clash and a "Save anyway" button.
- **Teams:** school and club contracts billed a flat monthly fee. Each month is invoiced on the contract's start day and emailed to the billing contact with a link to a branded invoice they can print or pay online (card or US bank transfer through Stripe). Record checks and ACH payments by hand, bill one-off extras, void mistakes. Overdue invoices get a reminder the day after they're due, then weekly, and show up on Today. Each team has a roster (paste the team list), its own sessions on the schedule, and attendance per athlete, with one tap to check in the whole team. One profile per athlete: everyone on a roster is a client (a new player gets a "Team only" profile with no membership; a client you already have is linked by choice or by Athlete ID, never by name alone), so team results, attendance, messages and progress reports all live on the athlete's own page. Add a parent to a team-only athlete from their page to give the family the parent portal.
- **Testing:** a preloaded library of 70+ tests from pro combines (NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB), soccer, youth and high school testing, plus the common force plate tests (countermovement jump, squat jump, drop jump, rebound jump, single-leg CMJ, isometric mid-thigh pull, isometric squat). Every metric has its unit and whether lower or higher is better; hide what you don't use or add your own. Plan testing days for individuals or a whole team roster, enter results by hand or with the built-in stopwatch (hand times stay labeled), and see personal records and progress on each athlete's page.
- **Athlete IDs:** every client and team roster player gets a permanent ID when their profile is created: first 3 letters of the first name, first 3 of the last name, and the year they joined (Ava Lopez → AVALOP2026; a second one that year → AVALOP2026-2). It never changes when a name does, is searchable, and is how uploads, devices and the API find the right athlete. Athletes created before IDs existed got theirs automatically.
- **Upload results (all or nothing):** download an Excel or CSV sheet with every athlete's ID already filled in and a column per test and attempt; fill it in; upload it (or paste rows, or a device export with Athlete IDs as the names). The whole sheet is checked before anything is saved: every row with results needs a real Athlete ID, a name on the row must match that ID's profile, every column must be a known test, every value must be a number inside the possible range for that test (catching numbers in the wrong column), no result may be entered twice, and dates can't be in the future. One problem anywhere and nothing is saved; you get a list by row and column of what to fix. Unusual-but-possible values (far off an athlete's record, or attempts that disagree) must each be confirmed. The sheet is checked again at save time and saved in a single transaction, so an upload is either fully in every profile or not in at all. Uploading the same sheet twice never double-counts.
- **Devices & imports:** Hawkin Dynamics force plates sync every 15 minutes. OVR, VALD, Swift, Freelap, Brower, Dashr, Rapsodo, radar guns or any spreadsheet import from a CSV, with columns matched automatically and remembered. Any other system can post to `POST /v1/results` with an API key. Results only land in a profile automatically when they carry an Athlete ID (in any field, including the name) or come from a device ID you've linked. Names are never matched on their own.
- **Waiting to be linked:** everything else goes to a queue (Testing → Waiting to be linked, also flagged on Today). Each sender's results are grouped with suggested athletes; you pick the athlete from a list that only accepts real Athlete IDs, link all of them or just the ones you tick, and optionally remember that device ID or name so its future results go straight in. Linking is all or nothing, and you can discard results instead. Linked device IDs are listed under Devices, where you can unlink them.
- **Hours & settings:** hours for privates and evaluations, each with a coach (parents book open times and see whose they are). Hours with a coach are blocked by anything that coach leads, at any place, and by sessions at that place with no coach; hours with no coach are blocked by anything at that place. A turned-off coach's hours aren't offered. **Time off** for a coach (coaches add their own) or the whole facility (owners) hides private and evaluation times on those days, on the portal and the Book now page too, and lists sessions that coach still leads then so you can give them a sub. Also your time zone, the late-cancel window and the waiver text.
- **Families:** athletes belong to a family with one or more parents. The family has one card that pays for every athlete, and one waiver signature. Athlete profiles hold birthday, sport, position, school, grad year, medical notes and an emergency contact.
- **Session packs and credits:** packs add either **group** or **private** credits. Members' group classes are covered by the membership; privates always use private credits or a paid drop-in. A full refund removes the pack's unused sessions.
- **Revenue by location:** see what the facility, each park and your mobile work bring in this month.
- **Programs:** exercise library with demo video links (YouTube, Vimeo or a direct video file), categories, search and filters (missing a video, not in a program) and where each exercise is used. The builder shows one week at a time: add, copy (to a run of weeks) and delete weeks, add and copy days, add exercises from a library search (sets, reps, tempo, rest, target RPE, a typed load and a cue as separate fields, filled in from their last use; a percent of a tested max for the weight), group them as supersets, circuits or blocks (shown together as A1, A2... in the builder, the athlete app and the weight-room screen; the app's rest timer follows the coach's rest), swap or remove them (with Undo), duplicate a program or start one as a copy. **Whole plan** shows the mesocycle: weeks down, days across, training phases (base, build, peak, deload, testing) as bands, each week's sets as a bar with its average percent of max and RPE, and **Progress weeks** builds a run of weeks from one by stepping sets, percent of max or RPE each week (or dropping them for a deload). Deleting a workout or a whole program keeps every athlete's logged workouts and sets in their history (client page, app and data download), just out of the program's numbers. Each program lists its clients with workouts done, what's next and when they last trained (amber after a quiet week); the Programs page shows workouts logged, who needs a check-in, who finished, and recent workouts with effort, sets, new bests and notes. Front desk sees it all read-only and can email the app link.
- **Demos in the flow:** an exercise's clip plays on its own, muted and looping, the moment the athlete opens it in the app, with the form-check button right under it; the weight-room screen plays the workout's clips in turn.
- **Form checks:** an athlete films one set from the exercise card in the app (Send a form check, up to 60 seconds) and the coach watches and answers on the client page or from Today, with a note (sent as a coach message too) and, if it helps, a clip of their own. Parents see the clips and answers on the Progress tab and can remove them. Clips live only in a private bucket of the owner's and are removed after 90 days (owner setting).
- **Wearable sync:** a parent (or a coach with the athlete's phone) presses Connect WHOOP or Connect Oura on the athlete's Progress tab, signs in on the provider's own page, the account's whole history comes in over the next day (no export files), and from then on recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep stages, strain, steps and workouts arrive a few times a day into the same Recovery & sleep view as a file import. Disconnect any time; what's on file stays. Apple Health and Garmin stay file imports (they don't offer this kind of link).
- **Card processing fee (optional, off by default):** Billing → Card processing fee lets the owner pass some of the card cost to the payer as its own line, ForActive-style: a percent plus a flat amount, on membership charges, card sales at the counter, pay links and the online store (any or all). Cash and check never carry it, bookings paid with the card on file from the portal, Book now or an offer link never carry it (the parent saw only the price), and school invoices are left out. The fee is always shown before paying: the counter, the pay page, the parent portal's store and plans and the Buy button all say the total and the fee, and receipts show the line. The panel carries a caution to check surcharge rules with an accountant or lawyer before turning it on.
- **API & integrations:** API keys with an access level (read only, read and send results, or full access) and a 30-day request log; webhooks with names, test events, delivery details, resend and secret rotation (only to public internet addresses); outbox search and send again; exercise video coverage; link to the full API spec.

**Accountability, Performance and Education**: athletes (in their app) and parents (Home tabs) get a daily check-in (sleep, hydration, soreness, energy, mood) with red flags for coaches, streaks, weekly goals, coach messages, test targets with progress, opt-in rankings by best result (no other names shown), and lessons, courses and assigned reading. Coaches run it from the Education screen and each client profile. The Education screen has Assigned, Library and Recent tabs: assign a lesson or course to several athletes at once (a team, a program, members, team-only athletes or picked by hand; athletes who already have it are skipped and named), see who hasn't started, opened, started or finished, change a due date or note, and send a reading reminder by email (Remind, or Remind overdue; at most one every 12 hours per assignment and per athlete, never to archived athletes, opted-out addresses or ended team contracts).

**Form checks in the plan:** on any exercise in a workout the coach turns on **Ask for a form check** (with what to film); the athlete's card shows the ask and the Send button up front, says when the clip went up, and Finish asks before finishing without it.

**Portal** (`/portal`; `/parent` opens the same page): parents and athletes sign in with the email on file. A 6-digit code is emailed each time, or they set a password inside the portal and use that. An athlete with their own email lands on their workouts (the app), a parent on the family portal, and someone who is both gets both. They see each athlete's upcoming sessions, membership and credits; book classes, clinics, privates and evaluations; join waitlists; cancel; hold a standing weekly spot (members); register for camps; buy packs and memberships with the family card; sign the waiver; and update athlete profiles and medical notes. It can be added to a phone's home screen like an app. Home shows each session's coach, place with directions, how it's paid, the late-cancel time and waitlist place; tap one to cancel or add it to a calendar, or subscribe to a private calendar link (reset any time). Book shows class details, blocks booking an athlete into two sessions at once, lets families pick a coach for privates and leave a note (emailed to the coach), and cancelling a private opens the time again and returns the credit or refund once. Progress has periods, test details, targets and share links. Programs shows the membership and lets parents ask to switch plans, pause or cancel: the owner is emailed and answers on the client page (nothing about billing changes by itself). Family has a to-finish list, card expiry, retrying a declined payment, removing the card, payments with receipts, adding a parent and signed-in devices. Sign-in locks for an hour after 15 wrong codes.

**One profile per athlete from the family's side:** a parent signing up or adding a child who already trains on a team can give the child's Athlete ID. When the name and full birthday match a profile with no family, it joins their family with its team results; otherwise a new profile is made and the owner is asked to check (the parent sees the same answer either way, and guesses are rate-limited). Owners merge two profiles of one athlete from Today or the client page: everything moves onto one profile in one step, the other Athlete ID keeps working, and it's in the activity log.

**Booking rules**
- A booking is covered by, in order: the membership (group classes only), a credit of the right type, or paying the drop-in price with the card on file. Coaches can also book now and collect at the session.
- Full sessions take a waitlist. When a spot opens, the next athlete moves up automatically and the family gets an email.
- Parents canceling inside the late-cancel window (default 12 hours) still use the session. Coaches can waive it.
- The waiver must be signed before a family books. Changing the waiver text asks every family to sign again.
- Age limits are enforced for parents; coaches can override.

**Email:** sign-in codes, booking confirmations, waitlist moves and cancellations. With `RESEND_API_KEY` set they're sent through Resend; without it they're logged to API & integrations → Email outbox, and in test mode the sign-in code is shown on screen.

**Athletes to check on and the weekly summary:** the app scores every active athlete for signs they're drifting away (no sessions in two weeks after coming regularly, coming less than half as often, nothing booked, repeated no-shows, stopped daily check-ins, and for owners only a failed payment or a trial with no visits) and lists the ones at risk on Today with the reasons. Every Monday at 7 am the owners get a summary email: money in against the week before, members joined and left, athletes to check on, open spots in the coming week, and up to three suggested actions. Preview it, send it now or turn it off in Hours & settings.

**Leads and follow-up (the CRM):** put the "Ask about training" link (`/start`) on your website and social pages. Every inquiry lands on the Leads tab, the owners get an email, and the family gets a thank-you right away with the sign-up link and the next step (a free evaluation). If they don't sign up, a short nudge goes out after 2 days and a last note after 7, then it stops. Parents who tick "text me" also get the first two as texts, and replying STOP ends them. Sign-ups that were started but never finished show up as leads after an hour and get the same follow-up. A lead moves to Signed up, Evaluation booked, Trial and Member on its own as the family creates an account, books an evaluation, starts a free trial and buys a membership or pack, and follow-up stops the moment they sign up. Front desk can add walk-ins and phone calls and works every lead with you. You can give a lead to a coach (they're emailed); coaches see and work only the leads you gave them. The Monday summary counts new inquiries and flags any nobody has reached out to.

Leads show as a list (search, stage, source, stale only, sort) or a pipeline board; every lead has a Move menu that works by keyboard and on a phone, and Lost asks why. Each lead shows how many days it has been in its stage, and a lead nobody has worked for a week turns amber. A lead's page has Call, Log a call, Add a note, Email and Text (with templates you edit under Leads → Settings; emails never go to an address that asked to stop and end with a stop link; texts only with the family's OK, never after STOP), contact preferences, tasks with due dates (overdue and today's show on the person's Today), "Make client" (the same rules as adding a client, or link a team profile by Athlete ID; nothing is retyped) and a timeline of everything that happened, including bookings and trials once they have an account. Adding a lead warns about the same email or phone as a lead, family or client. The client profile has the family's contact history with Email, Text, tasks and "Put back in pipeline". Owners also get Leads → Reports (by source, conversion, days to member, lost reasons, by period), Import & export (a CSV or Excel file, all or nothing, every problem by row and column) and Group messages, which can be texts and shows who is left out and why. In test mode, API & integrations → Texts can pretend a family texted back.

**Pay links and failed-payment recovery:** send a family a link to pay one thing by card without signing in: a membership payment that didn't go through, an unpaid session, a pack, or a set amount (Billing, or the Pay links panel on a client's page). Emails go to every parent and texts to parents who turned texts on; you can also copy the link and send it yourself. When a membership payment fails, the email and text now include a pay link automatically, and as soon as the family saves a new card the failed payment is charged right away instead of waiting for the next retry. Payments by link show in sales as "Pay link". If a family pays by link for something that was already paid another way, the money is refunded automatically and you get an email. Links stop working once paid, when you cancel them, or after 30 days. In test mode the pay page has a "Simulate payment" button; with Stripe it opens Stripe's secure card page.

**Self check-in:** athletes check themselves in for sessions they're booked on, from 30 minutes before the start until it ends, and the roster updates as they do. In Schedule → Hours & settings → Self check-in: **Door poster** prints a QR code for that location; parents scan it with their phone camera, sign in to the parent portal and tap their athlete. **Set up a tablet** gives a one-time link to open on a front-desk tablet; athletes tap their session and their name (shown as first name and last initial). An athlete whose session isn't paid is still checked in and asked to see the front desk. Remove a tablet or make a new door code there if a link or poster gets out. The QR codes are drawn by the app itself, no outside service.

**Text messages:** parents turn texts on in the parent portal (Family tab) with their mobile number and get a confirmation text. Then they get a reminder the day before each booked session (one per family, skipped for bookings made less than a day ahead), a text when an athlete moves off the waitlist, when you cancel a session, and when a membership payment doesn't go through. Replying STOP turns texts off, START turns them back on, HELP gets a short answer, and any other reply is emailed to the owners. Each kind can be turned off in Hours & settings. With `TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID`, `TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN` and `TWILIO_FROM` set they're sent through Twilio; without them they're only logged under API & integrations → Texts. `SMS_ONLY_TO` limits real texts on a staging copy.

**DP Coach iPhone app** (`ios/`): your pocket point of sale with Tap to Pay on iPhone. See `ios/README.md` to build it.

**Client app** (`/app?token=…`): each client gets a private link. It shows their next workout and progress through the program. Each exercise opens with its demo video, cues, last time's numbers and best weight; athletes log every set (weight and reps), with a rest timer between sets, rate how hard it was (1 to 10) and leave a note. Everything is kept on the phone as it's logged, so a workout done with no signal is sent when the connection returns, and never twice. The done screen shows sets, time, effort and new bests; the latest workout can be reopened for 2 hours. Coming up lists the next three workouts, and finished workouts open to show every set. Logging a workout on the weight-room screen and in the app counts once. Paused, canceled or archived athletes see a message instead of workouts.

**Billing rules**
- New subscriptions start with the plan's free trial; the first charge happens when it ends.
- Renewals charge monthly on the same day of the month.
- A failed charge makes the membership past due (the client keeps app access) and retries every 3 days. After 4 failed automatic attempts (the scheduled retries and the charge when a family saves a new card) the subscription is canceled; retries you or a parent start don't count.
- Pause stops charges and app access. Resume starts a new month and charges that day.
- Plan price changes apply from each client's next charge.

## The API

Everything the dashboard does goes through the same API your other systems use. Full spec: `GET /v1/openapi.json` (OpenAPI 3.1, importable into Postman, Zapier, Make and most tools).

```bash
curl http://localhost:3000/v1/clients -H "Authorization: Bearer dp_live_…"

curl -X POST http://localhost:3000/v1/clients \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer dp_live_…" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name":"Jordan Lee","email":"jordan@example.com","plan_id":"plan_…","program_id":"prog_…"}'
```

Lists return `{ "data": [...] }`. Errors return `{ "error": { "code": "...", "message": "..." } }` with a plain-English message. Money is in cents.

**Webhooks** fire on `client.created`, `client.updated`, `client.card_updated`, `subscription.created`, `subscription.updated`, `invoice.paid`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `program.assigned`, `workout.completed`, `sale.completed`, `sale.failed`, `sale.refunded`, `payment.disputed` and `session.checked_in`. Each request carries a `DP-Signature: t=<unix time>,v1=<signature>` header. Verify it on your side:

```js
import { createHmac, timingSafeEqual } from 'node:crypto';
function verify(rawBody, header, secret) {
  const { t, v1 } = Object.fromEntries(header.split(',').map((p) => p.split('=')));
  const expected = createHmac('sha256', secret).update(`${t}.${rawBody}`).digest('hex');
  const fresh = Math.abs(Date.now() / 1000 - Number(t)) < 300;
  return fresh && timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(v1), Buffer.from(expected));
}
```

Failed deliveries retry after 1 min, 5 min, 30 min, 2 hr and 12 hr.

```

    stripe-provider.js  Stripe: customers, Tap to Pay, readers, saved cards, refunds, webhooks
```

Security in place: passwords hashed with scrypt; sessions in HttpOnly cookies; API keys and sessions stored only as hashes; cross-site request protection; strict content security policy; parameterized SQL everywhere; input validation with clear messages; API keys can't manage other keys or webhooks.

## Payments: test mode and Stripe

With no `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`, the server uses built-in test payments: nothing is charged, taps are simulated from the dashboard, and each client has a "make card decline" switch.

With a Stripe key, everything runs through Stripe:
- **In person:** Tap to Pay on iPhone (in the DP Coach app) and Stripe smart readers at the front desk (sent from the dashboard, no app needed).
- **Saved cards:** from a tap (when the client agrees), or from a secure Stripe link you send them. Card details never touch this server.
- **Memberships:** this app runs the monthly billing clock and charges the saved card through Stripe.
- **Stripe webhooks** go to `https://your-domain/stripe/webhook`. Subscribe to `payment_intent.succeeded`, `payment_intent.payment_failed`, `payment_intent.canceled`, `payment_intent.amount_capturable_updated`, `checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `checkout.session.async_payment_failed`, `charge.refunded`, `charge.dispute.created`, `payment_method.automatically_updated`.

Use `sk_test_...` keys until you've run real test payments end to end. The server refuses to start with a live key while `DP_TEST_MODE=true`.

## Sign-up, import, agreements and emails

- **Family sign-up** (`/join`): a parent enters their details and their athletes (birthday required for age groups), agrees to the terms and privacy policy, and confirms their email with a 6-digit code. The family and athletes (with Athlete IDs) are created and the parent lands in the portal to sign the waiver and add a card. Protected by email confirmation, a hidden bot field and rate limits; it never reveals whether an email already has an account. Turn it on or off and copy the link in Hours & settings.
- **Client import** (Clients → Import from a spreadsheet): Excel or CSV template, one row per athlete. Siblings sharing a parent email become one family; a parent who already has an account gets the athlete added; adults use their own email. All or nothing: every problem is listed by row in one pass, and the preview shows the exact families and Athlete IDs before anything is saved. Optionally emails new families and adults their sign-in details.
- **Terms and privacy** (`/terms`, `/privacy`): your wording, set in Hours & settings. Placeholders are never shown to parents. Parents accept at sign-up; changing either asks every parent to accept again before their next booking or purchase. Every acceptance is recorded with name, version and time.
- **Data requests:** parents download everything held about their family (Family tab → Your data) or ask for deletion. Owners see requests on Today and in Settings, can download a family's data, and delete it by typing the family name. Deletion removes parents, athlete profiles, medical notes, test results, device links and saved cards; payment records stay without names.
- **Automatic emails:** welcome (sign-up, new client or import), receipts for sales and membership payments, a reminder 3 days before a free trial ends, and failed-payment notices to every parent with the next retry date. Each can be turned off in Hours & settings. Everything also appears in the Email outbox.

## Staff, security and backups

- **Roles:** Owner (everything), Coach (clients, schedule, testing, programs, point of sale; no billing, school contracts, refunds, API keys or staff) and Front desk (check-ins, sales, bookings, rosters, adding clients and families, entering test results). The server checks the role on every request; menus follow the role, and coaches and front desk never see revenue.
- **Staff accounts:** owners add staff under Settings. Each gets a one-time password by email and must choose their own at first sign-in. Owners can change roles, turn accounts off (signed out everywhere at once), reset passwords and unlock accounts. There is always at least one owner.
- **Sign-in protection:** five wrong passwords lock an account for 15 minutes; sign-in attempts and overall requests are rate-limited per address; parent sign-in codes are limited too (15 wrong codes lock that email for an hour). Staff who forget their password get an emailed link that works once, for 30 minutes (the same answer for any email).
- **Your account:** everyone sees the devices they're signed in on (sign out one or all others) and their recent sign-ins.
- **Hand-over:** when a coach leaves, Settings flags what they still lead and hands their upcoming sessions, classes and hours to another coach in one step. Clashes (the new coach is busy or off then) are warnings, as everywhere on the schedule: leave those sessions behind or hand over anyway.
- **Activity log:** every change, refused attempt and sign-in by staff, API keys and parents: who, what, which record, when, from where. Request contents are never stored.
- **Connection check:** Settings → Check my connection shows the X-Forwarded-For header your request arrived with, the connection address, the address the app decided is yours and the `TRUST_PROXY` setting, with one sentence on whether to change it. Open it on each server (staging and production) after a hosting change.
- **Backups:** a full copy of the database every day (last 30 kept), plus "Back up now" and downloads for the owner. Once off-site storage is set up (DEPLOY.md → Backups), each day's copy is also encrypted, sent to S3-compatible storage (Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2, AWS S3) and read back to prove it restores; Settings shows whether that is working, and a failed send alerts the owner like any background job.

## Parent progress reports

Parents see test results after you share a testing day ("Share with parents", with an optional note; families are emailed), or as soon as results are saved if you choose that in Hours & settings. The parent portal's Progress tab shows biggest improvements, new PRs, a trend line for every test, and growth. A printable, branded report (`/report.html`) is available to parents and coaches. For youth athletes with a birthday, sex, height, seated height and weight, the report estimates where they are relative to their growth spurt (Mirwald maturity offset, about ±1 year).

## Going to production

See **DEPLOY.md** for step-by-step instructions (Render or Fly.io, Docker, a persistent disk, your domain). In short: one instance with a persistent disk at `/data`, `DP_TEST_MODE=false`, `PUBLIC_URL=https://…`, `TRUST_PROXY` (`2` on Render, set in `render.yaml`; the Dockerfile's `true` means one proxy), your Stripe and Resend keys, and `ADMIN_EMAIL`/`ADMIN_PASSWORD` for the first start only. The app refuses to start with unsafe settings and says what to fix; `/healthz` reports health.

Later, as you grow: Postgres instead of SQLite (only `db.js` and a few date functions change), private video uploads (Mux or Cloudflare Stream), and emailed receipts and trial reminders.
