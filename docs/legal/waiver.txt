6. CONCUSSION ACKNOWLEDGMENT

A concussion is a brain injury caused by a bump, blow or jolt to the head or body. Signs include headache, dizziness, confusion, nausea, sensitivity to light or noise, balance problems, memory problems, drowsiness, mood changes and feeling "off". Symptoms may appear right away or hours or days later. Under Colorado's Jake Snakenberg Youth Concussion Act (C.R.S. § 25-43-101 and following), a coach who suspects an athlete has a concussion must remove the athlete from the activity at once and may not let the athlete return until a licensed health care provider has evaluated the athlete and given written clearance.

You agree to this. You agree to tell us about any concussion the athlete has had or has in another activity, and to report symptoms you see at home. You agree that our coaches may remove an athlete from a session for any safety reason, including a suspected concussion, heat illness or an injury, and that the session is not refunded or credited because of a safety removal.
