{{business_name}} PRIVACY POLICY

This policy explains what information Ad Astra Human Performance LLC, doing business as {{business_name}} ("we", "us", "our"), collects about you and your athletes, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, how we protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to our website, the parent and athlete portal, the athlete app, our online store and sales pages, our front desk and check-in tablets, our facility, our coaching, and our emails and texts. We wrote it in plain language because most of the people it protects are children and the parents who sign for them.

Our promises, in short:

We never sell your data or your athlete's data, we never share it for targeted advertising, and we never use it to build marketing profiles.
Your data is yours. You can download all of it, and you can delete your account and its personal data, at any time.
We collect only what we use, and we use your athlete's data only in their best interest: to coach them, keep them safe, and run the Services you asked for.
Parents own and control a minor's account. We always ask your permission before sharing your athlete's data with anyone other than the people and services needed to provide the Services, and before using their name or image for marketing.
Card details are stored by Stripe, never on our platform.

1. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Most of our athletes are under 18 and many are under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child under 13, and children under 13 never create their own logins or submit information to us on their own. Athlete accounts are created by a parent or legal guardian, who provides the athlete's information, consents to its use as described here, controls what is on the account, decides whether the athlete has their own sign-in, and can see everything the athlete does in the app. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13 without a parent's consent, tell us at {{contact_email}} and we will delete it. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Colorado Privacy Act's rules for minors' data, and Colorado law, whether or not their thresholds strictly apply to a business of our size.

2. WHAT WE COLLECT

Account and contact information: parent names, email addresses, phone numbers, relationship to the athlete, the family's name and, where you give it, your address; the athlete's own email and phone where an older athlete has them.

Athlete information: name, birth date, sex, sport, position, school, club, team, graduation year, emergency contacts, physician, and the health notes you give us (conditions, allergies, medications, injuries, surgeries, concussions, clearances and restrictions) so coaches can keep the athlete safe. Where a membership allows an injury pause or an in-season change, the proof you send (a provider's note, a roster or a schedule).

Training information: bookings and attendance, check-ins, memberships, packs, credits and balances, programs assigned, workouts logged (sets, reps, weights, effort, notes), test results and personal bests, maxes, progression steps, readiness check-ins (sleep, soreness, energy, mood, hydration), games and competition dates you enter, goals, badges and lessons read, and coaches' notes about training.

Clips and photos: form-check and sprint clips the athlete or parent sends us or a coach records for coaching, kept in private storage; photos taken for coaching purposes; and, only if you opt in through our Photo, Video and Media Release, photos and video for marketing.

Security video: cameras may record public areas of our facility (never restrooms or changing areas) for safety and security.

Wearable data: only if you connect a wearable (such as WHOOP or Oura) through the portal. We then receive daily recovery, sleep, heart rate variability, strain and workout summaries from that service for the athlete, until you disconnect it. We do not collect fingerprints, facial geometry or other biometric identifiers; video analysis in the app measures movement in a clip and does not identify anyone.

Messages: coach messages, team feed posts and comments, group board messages, and email and text messages between you and us, including your text-message consent and any STOP request.

Payment information: the card brand, last four digits, expiry date and funding type (credit or debit), a payment reference from Stripe, and the record of each charge, refund, credit, invoice and receipt. Full card numbers never reach us; they go directly to Stripe.

Technical information: the time and approximate device and browser used to sign in to the portal or app, so you can see where your account is signed in and sign other devices out; the IP address recorded when you sign the waiver or accept these terms, as evidence of your signature; and basic server logs kept for security. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers, and we do not place third-party analytics on pages built for athletes. The portal uses only the cookie needed to keep you signed in.

Inquiries: if you fill in our "ask about training" form, buy online, or sign up from a link, we keep what you entered and where the link came from (for example a flyer or our website) so we can follow up.

3. WHY WE USE IT

To provide the Services you asked for: scheduling, coaching, programs, testing, the app, the portal, memberships, invoices and receipts.
To keep athletes safe: health notes and emergency contacts for coaches, concussion and injury tracking, readiness suggestions, security recordings, and emergency response.
To coach better: results, logs, clips, wearable data and check-ins are used to adjust the athlete's own training and to show the athlete and parent their progress.
To run the business: payments, refunds, accounting, tax, daily money checks for errors, security, insurance, and the prevention of fraud and abuse.
To communicate with you: receipts, schedule changes, sign-in codes, reminders, coach messages, monthly progress reports, and, only if you have asked for them, texts and announcement emails. You can turn texts off by replying STOP and announcement emails off with the link in each one.
To meet legal obligations, such as keeping financial records, mandatory reporting of suspected child abuse or neglect, and responding to lawful requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build profiles for marketing, and we do not make automated decisions with legal or similar effects about you or your athlete. Readiness, progression and program suggestions are coaching aids that a coach or you can override.

4. WHO CAN SEE IT

Your family: the account holder sees everything on the account. An athlete with their own sign-in sees their own information. Other parents on the account see the family's information.

Our coaches and staff: owners and coaches see the athletes' training, health and contact information so they can coach safely. Front-desk staff see what they need to check athletes in, take payments and help families, and do not see coach-only notes. Every change staff make is logged.

Teams and groups: when an athlete is in a group or on a team roster, teammates see the athlete's first name and last initial and what the athlete writes on the shared board. They never see another athlete's results, attendance or health information. Rankings are shown only if the owner turns them on and the athlete opts in, and then only to the athletes who opted in.

Schools, clubs and team programs: when an athlete trains under a school, club or team contract, we share with that organization only what the contract needs, such as attendance and results for the sessions it pays for. We do not share health notes or family contact details with schools or clubs without your permission.

Partner physical therapists and other professionals: only when you ask us to, for example to confirm an injury pause or a return to training, and only what that needs.

Service providers who work for us: Stripe (payments), our email provider (Resend), our text-message provider (Twilio, only if texts are on), our hosting provider (Render), our storage provider (Cloudflare, for backups and private clips), Apple and Google (delivery of app notifications; the notification itself carries nothing personal), and the wearable services you connect (WHOOP, Oura). Where a coach uses an AI tool to read a program document or draft a progress note, only the document or the facts needed go to that provider (Anthropic), under terms that do not allow it to train on your data. Each of these providers may use your data only to provide its service to us and is bound by contract to protect it.

Nobody else, unless you say so or the law requires it. We ask your permission before sharing an athlete's data with anyone else, for example a doctor, physical therapist, school coach, college coach or recruiter. We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena or court order, to our insurers and lawyers in connection with a claim, to emergency responders, or to protect someone's safety, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it. If the business is sold, merged or reorganized, your data goes with it only to a successor who is bound by this policy.

5. PHOTOS, VIDEO AND YOUR ATHLETE'S IMAGE

Coaching clips and photos are private: seen only by our coaches and your family account, stored in a private bucket, never public. We do not use an athlete's name, image, voice, likeness, school or results in marketing, on our website, in print or on social media without your separate written permission in our Photo, Video and Media Release, which you can give in part and withdraw at any time by writing to {{contact_email}}. We never post an athlete's home address, a schedule that shows where a minor will be, or an athlete who is injured or in distress. Saying no never affects membership or coaching.

6. HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

While your account is open we keep what the account needs. Form-check and sprint clips are deleted automatically after the period the owner sets (90 days by default) unless you delete them sooner. Security recordings are kept for a short period (normally 30 days) unless needed for an incident. Sign-in codes expire within minutes; sign-in sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out. Wearable data stops arriving the moment you disconnect the device, and what has arrived is yours to delete with your account.

Accounts inactive for three years are deleted, except as described next, after we email you.

Transaction records (charges, refunds, credits, invoices, receipts) are kept for at least five years as accounting and tax law requires. When you delete your account, these records stay but your name and contact details are removed from them.

Signed waivers, accepted terms, incident and injury reports and concussion records are kept for as long as a claim could be brought, as the law and our insurer require, including until a minor athlete has turned 18 plus the limitation period, because they are evidence of your agreement and of what happened.

Backups are encrypted and kept for a limited period; deleted data drops out of backups as they age out.

7. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

You can do all of these yourself in the portal, or ask us at {{contact_email}}; we answer within 45 days and never charge for it:

See and download everything we hold about your family (Family tab, Download my data).
Correct anything that is wrong (edit the family and athlete details in the portal, or ask a coach).
Delete your account and your family's personal data (Family tab, or email us). We confirm by email first. Financial records stay without your name, and signed agreements and incident records stay as section 6 explains; everything else is removed.
Disconnect a wearable, delete a clip, turn texts off (reply STOP), unsubscribe from announcement emails, and sign out other devices, at any time.
Give, change or withdraw permission for marketing use of an athlete's image.
Opt out of the sale of your data and of targeted advertising: there is nothing to opt out of, because we do neither.

Colorado residents have these rights under the Colorado Privacy Act and may appeal a decision we make about a request by writing to {{contact_email}} with "Privacy appeal" in the subject; we respond within 45 days. We do not treat you differently for using your rights. Parents exercise these rights for athletes under 18.

8. SECURITY

We protect your data with encryption in transit, encrypted off-site backups, role-based access for staff, multi-factor protections on staff sign-in, audit logs of every change, rate limits and sign-in protections, and locked storage for any paper forms. Card numbers are handled only by Stripe. Staff see only what their role needs. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach that affects your personal information, we will tell you without unreasonable delay and within 30 days as Colorado law (C.R.S. § 6-1-716) requires, and we will notify the Colorado Attorney General where the law requires it.

9. TEXT MESSAGES

We send texts only to parents who turned them on (at sign-up, in the portal or by replying to us), for reminders, schedule changes and replies to your questions. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop, and HELP for help. Your consent to texts is not a condition of buying anything. Coaches message minors only through the app, through group threads or through the parent.

When we change this policy we post the new version at {{public_url}}/privacy with its date and ask the account holder to accept it in the portal before the next booking or purchase. If a change would let us use children's data in a new way, we ask for your consent first.
