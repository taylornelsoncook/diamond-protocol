# Diamond Protocol: project guide for Claude Code

## The business
Diamond Protocol ("Built Under Pressure") is a mobile sports-performance coaching business. It trains at its own facility, at clients' homes and in parks. Most clients are **parents paying for youth athletes**. Revenue mix: about 30% private training, 50% group classes, 20% other (camps and clinics, team contracts with schools and clubs billed a flat monthly fee, online programs, evaluations). The owner is not a developer: explain things in plain language and keep them informed of anything they need to do themselves.

## What this is
One web platform plus an iPhone app:
- **Coach dashboard** (`/`): Today, Schedule and rosters, Point of sale, Clients, Teams (school contracts and invoices), Testing, Billing, Programs, API & integrations, Staff & security.
- **Parent portal** (`/parent`), **family sign-up** (`/join`), **ask about training** (`/start`), **Book now page** (`/book`, embeddable with `/embed.js`; the only page other sites may frame), **terms and privacy** (`/terms`, `/privacy`), **athlete workout app** (`/app?token=`), **online store** (`/shop`), **printable progress report** (`/report.html`), **school invoice page** (`/invoice/:token`), **pay link page** (`/pay/:token`), **open-spot offer page** (`/spot/:token`), **self check-in** (`/here/:code` from the door poster `/poster.html`, tablet `/kiosk`), **weight-room screen** (`/tv`), **course certificate** (`/certificate#token`).
- **Open API** (`/v1/...`, reference at `/v1/openapi.json`) and a parent API (`/portal/api/...`).
- **iPhone app** `ios/` (SwiftUI "DP Coach": Today/rosters, Charge with Tap to Pay, Testing stopwatch, Clients). **Written but never compiled**: the first build on a Mac may need Swift fixes. `test/ios-contract.test.js` pins every field it reads.

Read `README.md` for features, `CHECKLIST.md` for the owner's to-do list, `DEPLOY.md` for going live, `ios/README.md` for the app.

## Rules we decided (don't undo these without asking the owner)
- **Athlete ID** (`AVALOP2026`: first 3 of first name + first 3 of last name + year joined, `-2` for duplicates) is permanent and ties all data together.
- **Results only land in a profile by Athlete ID, our internal IDs, or a device ID the coach linked by hand. Never guess by name.** Everything else waits in the queue (`results_queue`) for manual linking.
- **Uploads and imports are all or nothing:** check the whole file, list every problem by row and column, save nothing until clean, re-check at save time, save in one transaction. Unusual-but-possible values need explicit confirmation.
- **Roles** (Owner, Coach, Front desk) are enforced server-side in `services/security.js`; coaches and front desk never see money (`hideMoney` removes amounts from what they read, apart from what the counter needs to take a payment). Update the rules there for new endpoints.
- **Parents see test results only after a testing day is shared** (unless the setting says otherwise).
- **Terms/privacy placeholders start with `[`** and are never shown to parents or required.
- Deleting a family removes personal data but keeps payment records without names.
- Every change and sign-in is audit-logged without storing request bodies.
- **Coaches:** `coach_id` (a user, owner or coach, never front desk) on `class_series`, `class_sessions` (a sub overrides one session; handing a class over moves upcoming sessions still on the old coach) and `availability`. Open slots (`schedule.openSlots`, used by `/v1/slots`, the portal and `/book`): hours with a coach are blocked by anything that coach leads anywhere plus sessions at that place with no coach; hours with no coach by anything at that place; a turned-off coach's hours and days in `time_off` (a coach's, or the whole facility's when `user_id` is empty) aren't offered. `bookSlot` saves the session with the coach before charging, so a second booking of that coach at the same time (any place) is refused.
- **Open spots and trial offers** (`services/spots.js`): Today's list is every group class, clinic and camp day sold by the day with a spot left in the next 7 days, for every coach (coaches see it too, without prices; `?coach_id=me` for "My classes only"). The automatic job still only offers group classes and clinics within 30 hours. Trial offers (`/v1/sessions/:id/trial-offer`) are owner only in `security.js`; the price lives on `spot_offers.price_cents` (NULL = standard offer) and only `bookOffer` passes it to `schedule.book` (`offerPriceCents`), so no other booking is affected. A membership that covers the class still covers it; 0 books with coverage `none`. Price: whole cents, 0 to the drop-in (or $200 with none), and never more than the drop-in at booking time if it was lowered since. One booking per trial link, even when two athletes on it book at once; a trial booking never lands on the waitlist (the waitlist books at the usual price).
- **Owners see everything:** nothing on the owner's screens is limited to the signed-in user; per-coach views (`/v1/coach-summary`, owner only; Schedule `?coach=`) are filters the owner chooses.
- **Archived clients** (`clients.archived_at`) are left out of `/v1/clients` (unless `?archived=true|all`), dashboard counts and every job that contacts families (reminder texts, open-spot offers, review asks, announcement emails, at-risk). New lists or jobs that reach clients must leave them out too. Archiving is refused with a membership and cancels upcoming bookings only with `confirm: true`; archived clients can't be booked or given a membership. "Active client" = not archived and membership `active` or `trialing` (`clients.clientCounts`, `status=current`), the same on Today and the client list.
- **Staff notes** (`client_notes`): coach-only notes never reach front desk (filtered server-side); in the family export, removed on deletion.
- Plain-English UI copy, sentence case, active voice. Errors say what to fix.
