# Diamond Protocol

Diamond Protocol is a coaching brand built on one idea: pressure makes diamonds. Everything we make — the coach dashboard, the client app, decks, pages and social posts — should feel forged: dark, metallic, precise, with green as the mark of progress. Tagline: **Built under pressure.**

## Voice

Talk like a good coach: direct, calm, specific. Plain verbs, short sentences, sentence case in running text. Say what happens ("Retry charge", "Log set", "Finish workout"), never hype. Celebrate effort with facts ("Workout logged. Your coach can see it.") rather than exclamation marks or emoji.

## Logo

Use the supplied files in Logos only; never redraw, recolor or stretch the mark. The full lockup (diamond, DIAMOND PROTOCOL, tagline) is for covers, splash screens and first slides. The diamond mark alone is for app headers, favicons and small spaces. Always place it on `black` or `ground`; give it clear space of at least the height of one crown triangle. The D is silver, the P is green — that split is the brand.

## Color

The palette is steel on black with one green. Build every screen on `ground` with `surface` panels outlined in `line`. Text is `steel`, captions `steel-muted`. `green` is the only fill color for actions — one primary button per view. Green text on dark uses `green-bright`, never `green` (too dark to read). Status: `green-deep`/`green-soft` for active and paid, `amber-deep`/`amber` for failed or at risk, `surface-raised`/`steel-muted` for paused. No gradients, no blue, no extra accent colors.

## Type

`display` (Chakra Petch) is the squared, engineered face that echoes the wordmark: page titles in uppercase with 0.06em tracking, big numbers, workout names. `sans` (IBM Plex Sans) for everything people read. `mono` for API keys, endpoints and code. The `tagline` style — small, uppercase, widely tracked — is for the brand line and nothing else.

## Shape and layout

Squared and structural: `radius-sm` on controls, `radius-md` on panels, `radius-pill` only for status badges. Separate with 1px lines, not shadows. Touch targets at least 44px. Motifs come from the diamond: facets, triangles and sharp angles — used sparingly, never as wallpaper.

## Iconography

Thin stroke icons (1.6px) in `steel` or `steel-muted`, square line joins. No emoji, no filled cartoon icons.
